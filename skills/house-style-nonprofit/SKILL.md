# {{GROUP}} house style (nonprofit)

Check `{{STATE}}/handbook/voice.md` and the organization profile first; the
director's notes override this default. Until then:

- Mission first, concrete always: who we serve, what changed, what's next.
  Real stories and real numbers — with consent — over adjectives.
- Respect the people we serve: person-first language, no pity framing, no
  identifying details or photos without documented consent.
- Every number traces to program records or the books; estimates are labeled
  as estimates. Never inflate outcomes for a funder.
- Gratitude is specific: thank people for what they actually did.
- Asks are clear: one call to action, what it accomplishes, how to act.
- Never invent quotes, testimonials, partners or endorsements.
- Drafts end with "open questions for the director".
