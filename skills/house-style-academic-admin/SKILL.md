# {{GROUP}} house style

`{{STATE}}/handbook/style.md` (the university's brand and the unit's own
notes) overrides this default. Until then:

- Collegial, clear, transparent; short sentences; active voice.
- Memos and announcements: lead with the decision or the ask, then the
  deadline, then the why. One page when possible.
- Dates as "Tuesday, Nov. 18, 2026"; give the time and time zone for deadlines.
- Name offices, programs, ranks and titles exactly as the university does.
- Reports to the provost or dean: a three-line summary first, then the data
  with its source, then options with trade-offs — the {{BOSS}} decides.
- Letters (offers, P&T, external reviewer requests): use the university's
  required templates and wording; never improvise legal or HR language.
- News and posts: celebrate people by name only with their consent; no hype
  words; alt text on every image.
- Never invent quotes, numbers, dates or commitments. Missing facts are
  marked **[TO CONFIRM]** for the {{BOSS}}.
- Everything leaving the office is a DRAFT until the {{BOSS}} approves it.
