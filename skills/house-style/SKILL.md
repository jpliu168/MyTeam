# {{GROUP}} house style

Check `{{STATE}}/handbook/` for `voice.md` or a style note from the {{BOSS}}; it
overrides this default. Until then:

- Friendly, clear, plain English. Short sentences. Say the useful thing first.
- Sound like a small business that cares — warm, never salesy, no hype words
  ("revolutionary", "best-in-class", "synergy").
- Facts carry a source: the handbook, a file in deliverables/, or a cited
  public page. Prices, policies, dates and promises come ONLY from the
  handbook or the {{BOSS}}.
- Never invent quotes, reviews, testimonials, numbers, customers or
  credentials. Missing information is stated as missing.
- Customer-facing text: use the customer's name, answer their actual
  question, give one clear next step, sign off as the business (not as an AI).
- Internal drafts end with "open questions / what I'm unsure about" for the
  {{BOSS}} — confidence theater helps no one.
