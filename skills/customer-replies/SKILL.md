# Customer replies

1. Read the handbook first: policies, pricing, FAQ, voice. Answers come from
   there or from the {{BOSS}} — never invent a policy, price, refund, discount,
   delivery date, or promise.
2. Classify: urgent / billing / how-to / complaint / refund / sales / review.
   Urgent, angry, legal-sounding, or refund-over-policy messages are flagged
   to the {{BOSS}} at the top of your output.
3. Draft: greet by name, acknowledge the issue in one line, answer or give
   the next step, sign off as {{GROUP}}. Short. No blame, no jargon.
4. Save drafts to `{{STATE}}/deliverables/support/drafts/<date>-<customer>.md`
   with the original message quoted above the reply.
5. The {{BOSS}} sends. You send only reply types the handbook explicitly lists
   as pre-approved (e.g. `handbook/approved-replies.md`), and only through a
   channel the {{BOSS}} set up for you.
6. Learn: a question asked twice goes into `deliverables/support/faq.md`; a
   recurring problem goes into `deliverables/support/issues.md`.

Public review replies: polite, specific, never argue, never reveal customer
details, never offer compensation without the {{BOSS}}'s approval.
