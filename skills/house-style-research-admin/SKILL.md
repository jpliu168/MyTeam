# {{GROUP}} house style

`{{STATE}}/handbook/style.md` (the university's brand and news style)
overrides this default. Until then:

- Clear, plain, upbeat; short sentences; active voice. AP style for news.
- Lead with what the reader can do: deadline, amount, eligibility, link.
- Dates as "Tuesday, Nov. 18, 2026"; always say the time zone for deadlines.
- Name sponsors and programs exactly as the sponsor does; link the official page.
- News releases: headline, one-sentence lede, the finding or award in plain
  language, a quote placeholder **[QUOTE — to confirm with researcher]**,
  sponsor acknowledgment with award number, "About" line, media contact.
- Social posts: one idea, one link, tag the unit and sponsor accounts listed
  in the handbook, alt text for every image. No hype words ("groundbreaking").
- Never invent quotes, numbers, award amounts or titles. Missing facts are
  marked for the {{BOSS}}.
- Everything public is a DRAFT until the {{BOSS}} approves it.
