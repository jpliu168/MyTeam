# {{GROUP}} house style

`{{STATE}}/handbook/style.md` (your own notes and your advisor's preferences)
overrides this default. Until then:

- Plain, direct scientific English. Short sentences. Active voice ("we show",
  not "it is shown"). No filler ("novel", "cutting-edge", "it is worth
  noting").
- Claims carry evidence: a number, a citation, or a pointer into
  deliverables/. A claim you cannot back gets flagged, not polished.
- Never invent citations, data, quotes, or reviewer text. Missing
  information is stated as missing.
- US spelling; SI units; define every acronym at first use in each chapter.
- Figures: the caption states the finding, not just the content.
- Emails to the advisor or committee: the ask or decision first, then the
  deadline, then the context; one screen; attach, don't paste, long drafts.
- Thesis/dissertation: follow the graduate school's template exactly
  (handbook/program.md) — margins, headings, front matter, citation style.
- Drafts end with an "open questions / what I'm unsure about" list for the
  {{BOSS}}.
- Everything is a DRAFT until the {{BOSS}} sends or submits it.
