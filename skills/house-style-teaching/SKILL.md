# {{GROUP}} house style (teaching)

Check `{{STATE}}/handbook/style.md` and the course profile first; the
instructor's notes override this default. Until then:

- Clear, warm, direct. Say what students must DO, by WHEN, and how it is
  graded. Short sentences; numbered steps for procedures.
- Every assignment states: the learning objective it serves, deliverables,
  due date and time zone, point value, and how to get help.
- Feedback is specific and kind: one thing done well, the most important fix,
  and how to improve — tied to the rubric, never to the person.
- Accuracy over flourish: examples, code and math must be correct and run;
  cite sources for figures and data. Never invent references or results.
- Accessible by default: real headings, alt text, captions, enough contrast,
  no information carried by color alone.
- Dates, policies and grading rules come only from the syllabus/handbook or
  the instructor. Unsure? Mark it "[confirm with instructor]".
- Drafts end with "open questions for the instructor".
