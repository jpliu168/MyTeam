# {{GROUP}} house style

Check `{{STATE}}/handbook/` for a style note from the PI; it overrides this
default. Until then:

- Plain, direct scientific English. Short sentences. Active voice ("we show",
  not "it is shown"). No filler ("novel", "cutting-edge", "it is worth
  noting").
- Claims carry evidence: a number, a citation, or a pointer into
  deliverables/. A claim you cannot back gets flagged, not polished.
- Never invent citations, data, quotes, or reviewer text. Missing information
  is stated as missing.
- US spelling; SI units; define every acronym at first use.
- Figures: caption says the finding, not just the content ("X outperforms Y
  at long leads", not "comparison of X and Y").
- Drafts end with an explicit "open questions / what I'm unsure about"
  list for the PI — confidence theater helps no one.
